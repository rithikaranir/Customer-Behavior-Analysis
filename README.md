# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing patterns, spending behavior, subscription trends, and product performance using Python, SQL, and Power BI. The project transforms raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL-based business queries, and interactive dashboards.

The objective is to help businesses understand customer preferences, identify high-value segments, evaluate revenue drivers, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior and spending patterns.
- Identify factors influencing customer retention and subscriptions.
- Evaluate product and category performance.
- Measure the impact of discounts and promotions on sales.
- Develop interactive dashboards for business reporting.
